Method 1: Check Your Balance Online (Website Method)

The fastest way for a ratibi fab balance check is often online. You do not need to download an app for this method. You just need a phone, tablet, or computer with internet access. This website is the official portal for Ratibi prepaid card users. You should use this if you only have a salary card and not a full bank account with FAB.

Step by step:

  1. Go to the official website: Open your browser and visit the official FAB prepaid card balance enquiry portal. The address is ppc.magnati.com/ppc-inquiry. This link is provided by FAB for existing Ratibi cardholders.
  2. Enter your card number: On the page, you will see a box asking for your card number. Look at your physical Ratibi card. Type the full 16 digit number from the front of the card into this box.
  3. Enter your card ID: Next, you need to enter your Card ID. This information is also printed on your card. It might be your date of birth or a specific ID number given to you by the bank.
  4. Complete the security check: Many websites have a security step to prove you are not a robot. You will usually need to type a few letters or numbers from a picture. This is called a captcha verification.
  5. Click Go or Submit: Once you have filled in all the details, click the button to submit your request. The page will then take a few seconds to process your information.
  6. View your balance: Your balance will appear on the screen. You will see exactly how much money is currently available on your Ratibi card. It is that simple.

important Never enter your full card number, PIN, or password on a third party website. Always use the official FAB or Magnati links to keep your money safe.

Method 2: Check Balance Using the FAB Mobile App

Another excellent way to complete your ratibi fab balance check is by using the FAB mobile app. However, there is one thing you need to know. The standard FAB Mobile app is usually for people with regular bank accounts. If you try to log in with only your Ratibi card details, it might not work.

Try these steps:

  1. Download the app: Go to the Google Play Store (for Android) or the App Store (for iPhone). Search for FAB Mobile and download the official app.
  2. Register or log in: Open the app. If you have an account, log in with your username and password. If not, you will need to register using your Emirates ID and card details.
  3. Find the balance: Once you are logged in, look at the dashboard. Your balance should appear on the main screen. If you do not see it, look for an option that says Accounts or Check Balance.
  4. Check the card section: If you see your Ratibi card listed, click on it. The app should show you the available balance and your recent transactions.

Note: For most Ratibi users, the website portal is the correct way to check online. If the FAB Mobile app does not work for you, do not worry. Just use the website link from Method 1.

Method 3: Check Balance via SMS

If you do not have internet access, there is a very simple way to perform a ratibi fab balance check. You can use your phone SMS system. This works on any basic mobile phone. It is fast and does not cost much.

Send an SMS:

  1. Open the messaging app on your phone.
  2. Create a new text message.
  3. Type the word BAL.
  4. Send this message to the number 6667.
  5. Within a few seconds, you will receive a reply SMS. This message will contain your current balance.

Cost: This service is not usually free. The bank or your telecom provider may charge a small fee per message. It costs about 0.30 AED per SMS.

Note: If you do not receive a reply, it might mean your mobile number is not registered with the card. In that case, you will need to contact your employer or FAB support to update your details.

Method 4: Check Balance at an ATM

The traditional way to check your money is at the ATM. This is a reliable method for a ratibi fab balance check. It is perfect if you are already out and near a cash machine.

  1. Insert your Ratibi card into the ATM machine.
  2. Enter your 4 digit Personal Identification Number (PIN).
  3. From the menu on the screen, select Balance Inquiry.
  4. The machine will process your request.
  5. Your current balance will appear on the screen. You can print a receipt if you want to keep a record of the amount.

This method is safe and secure. It is a great backup if the internet is down or you do not have your phone with you. Checking your balance at a FAB ATM is usually completely free.

Why is My Balance Not Showing?

Sometimes, you try to do a ratibi fab balance check, but the balance is wrong or missing. There could be a few reasons for this. Here are the most common ones:

  • Salary not processed: Your employer might not have sent the salary yet. Sometimes, the payment is delayed. If the company submits the payroll late, your money will be late. In the UAE, the Wage Protection System (WPS) requires companies to pay on time. However, processing can take 24 to 48 hours.
  • Bank cut off times: If the employer sends the file after the bank cut off time, it will be processed the next working day.
  • Processing issues: The WPS file might have an error. If a single detail is wrong, the bank might reject the entire file. This will delay your salary.
  • Delays in international transfers: If the company sends money from outside the UAE, it can take longer. International wires often take 2 to 4 business days to appear on your card.
  • Pending transactions: If you paid for something, the money might be on hold. This means the merchant has reserved the money, but the transaction is not final yet. It will reduce your available balance.

What to do: If it has been more than 2 days after your official payday, contact your company HR department. They can check if there is a problem with your file. If HR says they have processed it, call FAB customer support.

How to Stay Safe

When you check your balance, always be careful with your information. Scammers are everywhere. Here are some simple rules to protect your money:

  • Use official sources: Only use the links from the official FAB website or the Magnati portal. Do not use random websites that ask for your card details.
  • Keep your PIN secret: Never tell anyone your ATM PIN or password. The bank will never ask for this information.
  • Beware of fake SMS: Do not click on links in SMS messages that claim to be from FAB unless you are sure they are legitimate.
  • Check the website address: Always look at the website address (URL) to make sure it is the official one (ppc.magnati.com).

By following these tips, you can keep your hard earned money safe.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. How do I check my Ratibi card balance online?

Go to the official FAB prepaid card balance enquiry portal at ppc.magnati.com/ppc-inquiry. Enter your 16 digit card number and your Card ID. Click Go to see your balance.

2. Can I check my FAB Ratibi balance using the FAB Mobile App?

It depends. The FAB Mobile app is primarily for regular bank accounts. Ratibi prepaid card users often need to use the specific online portal mentioned above. If you can log in to the app, you can check it there; otherwise, use the web portal.

3. Is there a fee to check my balance?

Checking your balance at a FAB ATM or on the FAB website is free. However, checking your balance via SMS (BAL to 6667) usually costs a small fee, approximately 0.30 AED per message.

4. I sent an SMS but got no reply. What should I do?

This often means your mobile number is not registered with the bank for the Ratibi card. Contact your employer HR department or call FAB customer support at 600 525 500 to update your mobile number.

5. Why is my salary not showing on my card?

Your employer may have delayed the payment. Also, the Wage Protection System (WPS) transfers can sometimes take 24 to 48 hours to process. Contact your HR department if it has been more than 2 days after payday.
